# Surface brief — Home (site-wide direction)

## Scope and mode

The public site's shared visual world: Home plus About, Committee, Events, Partners, Membership, Gallery, Blog, Contact and the shared Navbar/Footer. Visitor mode: Persuade. The practice arena stays Operate and only inherits tokens, never the performance.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- Primary success audience: corporate recruiters/partners deciding whether JKKNIU-SDC is a credible student-talent pipeline; students deciding to join are the second reader.
- Job: judge credibility within seconds, then act. Actions: "Partner with us" (primary for partners), "Apply for Membership" (primary for students).
- Proof: BizCase 2020 (370+ teams, 65+ universities), BizCase 2026 (1 Lakh BDT prize pool), JKKNIU Job Fair 2023, MoU partners AKIJ Resource / US-Bangla Airlines / The Business Standard / Excellence Bangladesh, 500+ members, 100+ events, established March 13, 2018.
- Constraints: factual copy preserved (stats carried as-is, never amplified); no invented testimonials, press, or partner logos; logo asset `/jkkniu-sdc transparent logo.png` binding; 44px touch targets; responsive to 390px.

The Job Fair Hall — the locked sketch `.impeccable/mocks/decision/assigned.html` is the composition the build matches; code-led build. Memorable moment: the main-stage banner beside the black hall counter whose numbers visibly tick.
